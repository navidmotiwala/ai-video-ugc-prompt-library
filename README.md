## How to use this library

1. **Choose your objective.** Define the product, audience, platform, and desired result.
2. **Prepare reference images.** Label each product and character reference clearly.
3. **Create the character.** Keep appearance, outfit, and reference images consistent.
4. **Plan each shot.** Describe action, setting, camera, lighting, audio, and duration.
5. **Generate short clips.** Review output for continuity, product accuracy, and visual artifacts.
6. **Edit the final video.** Join clips, verify captions and claims, and export for your platform.

## Reusable prompt input checklist

- Goal and target audience
- Product or subject and verified details
- Character and reference images
- Scene location, lighting, and style
- Shot framing and camera movement
- Dialogue, language, and audio
- Aspect ratio and target duration
- Continuity requirements
- Exclusions and quality checks

## Important notes

The prompts are templates, not guaranteed outputs. Image references, audio, JSON formatting, and maximum video length depend on the model used. Check generated text, hands, logos, product packaging, dialogue, and transitions. Use a person's likeness or voice only with appropriate permission. Avoid misleading product claims.
