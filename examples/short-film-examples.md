Personality: Ambitious, hardworking, emotional, determined

Physical Appearance: Medium brown skin, lean build, youthful appearance

Hair Style: Short black textured hair

Eye Color: Dark Brown

Clothing: Black hoodie, blue jeans, white sneakers

Special Features: Expressive eyes, friendly face

No identity changes

## STEP 2 ,  Story Foundation

#### Genre

Motivational Drama

#### Theme

Never Giving Up On Your Dreams

#### Story Length

60 Seconds

#### Story Structure

Beginning: Sandeep is a struggling content creator who works late every night but gets very few views.

Middle: Despite repeated failures and disappointment, he keeps learning and creating new content.

Climax: One of his videos suddenly goes viral overnight.

Ending: Aarav achieves success and inspires others to keep chasing their dreams.

Emotion: Determined

### Scene 2

Duration: 10 Seconds
