##### Dialogues/Action(List) : -

Dialogue/Action(List) Format

Dialogue 1 - (Jo Bhi Tume Likhna Ho)

Action 1 - Action while saying the dialogue

Dialogue 2 - (Jo Bhi Tume Likhna Ho.)

Action 2 - Action while saying the dialogue

#### For example:-

Dialogue 1 - ("Okay, so mujhe ye product recently mila tha aur ab main kaafi regularly use kar rahi hoon.”.)

Action 1 - (She picks up the product and looks at it.)

Dialogue 2 - ("Sach bolun to mujhe itna achha result expect nahi tha, but I'm actually impressed.”.)

Action 2 - (She glances between the product and the camera.)

Dialogue 3 - ("Isliye socha aap logon ke saath bhi share kar doon, maybe aapko bhi pasand aa jaye.”.)

Action 3 - (She holds the product toward the camera and smiles naturally.)
